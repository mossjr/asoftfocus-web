A Soft Focus — Piano, Guitar & Powerhouse Vocal Duet

Short Bio (50 Words): Based in Hervey Bay, QLD, A Soft Focus is the real-life husband-and-partner musical duo of UK powerhouse vocalist/actress Sammy-Jay Cobar and Canadian-Australian pianist, guitarist, and theatre sound designer Kyle Davis. Offering full-band JamZone backing sets, intimate acoustic storytelling, and solo dinner piano across the Fraser Coast.

Technical Rider & Stage Footprint

What We Bring (Self-Contained)

  • Yamaha Stage Piano (88-Key Weighted) + Acoustic-Electric Guitar
  • Professional Front-of-House PA System & Foldback Monitoring
  • Shure Wireless/Vintage Vocal Microphones & Stands
  • iPad / MacBook JamZone Multi-Track Backing System (for Full Band sets)
  • Compact Warm Stage & Mood Lighting (programmed by Kyle, Z-Pac Theatre Designer)

What We Need From the Venue

  • Minimum Stage Space: 2.5m wide x 2m deep (compact footprint fits any corner or deck)
  • Access to 1–2 standard 240V power outlets within 5m of performance area
  • Weather-protected / shaded area for outdoor afternoon sessions
  • Load-in & soundcheck: 45–60 minutes prior to downbeat (15 mins for Solo Piano)
