How we look after your video and details

  • What we collect: your name, any Instagram or Facebook handle you give us, your email if you add one, which gig you filmed, and your video. To stop spam and abuse we also keep a small technical record: the time, your approximate country, your browser type and a scrambled (one-way) version of your IP address.
  • Why: to watch your clip, possibly use parts of it in A Soft Focus videos, credit and tag you, and contact you if we need to.
  • Where it's stored: privately with Cloudflare and Google Drive, which may use servers outside Australia. Your video is never public unless we choose to use part of it in one of our own edits.
  • How long: we delete clips we don't use after 12 months.
  • Your choices: email bookings@asoftfocus.com at any time to have your video removed or to stop us using it. If we've already posted it, we'll take our post down or remove your clip from future edits.
  • Other people in your video: if anyone in the crowd asks us to, we'll blur or cut them. We avoid featuring children.
  • Location data: phones can store where a video was filmed inside the file. We never publish your original file, only our own edited versions.
