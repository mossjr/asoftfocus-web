The Story Behind the Music

Meet Sammy-Jay & Kyle

A real-life couple who met in a theatre rehearsal room in February 2024 and haven't stopped making music together since.

The Singer • Vocals & Storytelling

Sammy-Jay

Originally from the United Kingdom and calling Australia home for the past three years, Sammy-Jay is a powerhouse vocalist, international performer, and passionate Drama & Film teacher in Hervey Bay.

Equally at home delivering roof-raising anthems or whisper-soft acoustic storytelling, she is also a familiar face on the Z-Pac Theatre and Brolga Theatre stages.

The Piano Man • Keys, Guitar & Sound

Kyle

Originally from Canada and a proud Australian citizen after 17+ years down under, Kyle is a versatile multi-instrumentalist across piano, keys, acoustic guitar, and drums.

Also an accomplished theatre technician and designer at Z-Pac Theatre, Kyle brings crystal-clear live sound engineering, rich musical arrangements, and effortless solo piano elegance to every gig.
